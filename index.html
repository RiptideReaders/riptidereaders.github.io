Riptide Readers

A book club for Riptide Masters swimmers

Every month, something new lifts us up. This is where we keep the schedule, throw out ideas, and vote on what's next.

This isn't a traditional book club. No meetings, no discussions to prep for. Just something fun to talk about at practice and in the locker room.

Back up the site

Exports one file with everything: the schedule, suggestions and votes, reviews and ratings, announcements and RSVPs, and the member list. On your phone, tap Export, then tap again when it says the backup is ready, and choose Save to Files. It includes members' emails and phone numbers, so don't share it.

Restoring puts everything in the file back, overwriting anything with the same ID. It never deletes things that aren't in the file.
